Health Clinics nearby

North Fulton Regional Health Center (Fulton County Board of Health)

The North Fulton Regional Health Center, operating as part of the Fulton County Board of Health, is a major public health facility located at its primary North Fulton campus—the Fulton County Health & Human Services – North facility at 4700 North Point Parkway in Alpharetta (which opened in April 2026). The center provides North Fulton residents with essential health and community resources under one roof, including public health nursing, adult and pediatric dental care, WIC nutrition services, vital records, behavioral health and developmental disabilities support, and senior services. Additional regional access points include related facilities like the North Fulton Health Center in Sandy Springs.

Good Samaritan Health Center of Gwinnett

Good Samaritan Health Centers of Gwinnett provides affordable general medical, primary care, and specialty health services alongside routine dental care—including fillings, extractions, restorations, and dentures—for low-income and uninsured families. Dedicated to meeting the physical, mental, and spiritual needs of every patient, the clinic also offers deeply discounted prescription medications through an in-house dispensary as well as faith-based biblical counseling for individuals, couples, and families.

CPACS COSMO Health Center

CPACS Cosmo Health Center provides affordable, high-quality primary care designed to meet the needs of a diverse community through preventive medical care—such as routine wellness checks, screenings, vaccinations, and family planning—alongside comprehensive dental services for patients of all ages. In addition to physical and oral health, the center supports long-term wellness through personalized behavioral health counseling for mental and emotional challenges, as well as a stigma-free Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT) program that combines FDA-approved medications with whole-person counseling to help individuals recover from opioid and substance use disorders.

Overall

Together, these three health centers ensure that uninsured, low-income, and diverse families across North Fulton and Gwinnett counties have accessible, affordable healthcare close to home. By combining the North Fulton Regional Health Center’s public health and nutritional programs in Alpharetta with Good Samaritan’s charitable medical, dental, and medication services in Norcross and CPACS COSMO’s preventive and behavioral health care in Peachtree Corners, this network supports the physical, dental, and mental well-being of the entire community.
