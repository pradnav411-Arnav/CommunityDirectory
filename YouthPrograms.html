Youth Programs

Events for Younger children to attend

Lowe's Kids Workshops

Lowe's offers free monthly workshops where children can learn basic building skills through fun, hands-on projects. Kids get to use tools, follow instructions, and create something they can take home. For example, children might build a wooden toy train or a small birdhouse. These workshops encourage creativity, problem-solving, and teamwork while introducing children to DIY projects.

Fulton County Library System

The Fulton County Library System offers free educational and recreational activities for children of different ages. Local branches, including Ocee Library and Spruill Oaks Library, host programs such as storytime, arts and crafts, reading activities, and STEM workshops. For example, children can participate in a STEM & Nature Club to explore science through hands-on activities. These programs help children develop creativity, curiosity, and learning skills in a welcoming environment.

Autrey Mill Nature Preserve

Autrey Mill Nature Preserve offers families a free opportunity to explore nature, wildlife, and local history in Johns Creek. Children can walk along forest trails, observe animals, and visit educational exhibits at the Visitor Center. For example, families can take a nature walk to discover native plants, wildlife, and historic landmarks. The preserve encourages outdoor exploration and helps children learn about protecting the environment.

Overall

Together, these services connect children and families with free, fun, and educational activities in the Johns Creek and Alpharetta area. From hands-on building workshops and creative library events to outdoor nature exploration, these programs provide opportunities for children to learn new skills, explore their interests, and have fun in a welcoming environment.
