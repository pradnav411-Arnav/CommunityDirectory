HiFive Tutoring

HiFive is a personalized tutoring service offering expert support across K–12, undergraduate, and graduate-level subjects and exams. We help students stay ahead of their curriculum, develop essential study skills, and improve GPAs and test scores—with proven results.

The David Academy

The David Academy exists to help prepare students to make the transition from high school to college. Families come to The David Academy for individualized solutions that deliver real results, ranging from ACT/SAT Test Prep Coaching to consulting services for the Admissions Process to assistance with the Financial Aid Process. With over 20 years of experience, we use a proven method, The David Academy Method… that breaks the classroom model in favor of a proven one-on-one style of coaching that delivers results. Students from around the U.S. can enroll at The David Academy thanks to our convenient and effective all-virtual model.

Mathnasium

Unlike private tutors, Mathnasium's curriculum and methodology have been proven through the success of hundreds of thousands of students. Private tutors are reactive, Mathnasium is proactive; Mathnasium builds each child's needed mathematical skills methodically and comprehensively. Backed by the extensive training and resources of a global organization, the instructors deliver a superior method, typically at a lower cost.

Overall

Together, these three tutoring centers provide comprehensive academic support for students across Johns Creek and Alpharetta. Combining HiFive Tutoring’s multi-subject curriculum and study skill coaching, The David Academy’s one-on-one ACT/SAT test prep and college admissions guidance, and Mathnasium’s proactive math skill-building methodology, this network equips learners at every level with the tools they need to boost their confidence, improve grades, and succeed academically
